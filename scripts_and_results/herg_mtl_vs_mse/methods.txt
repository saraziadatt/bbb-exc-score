An hERG mixed-label benchmark combined exact ChEMBL IC50 measurements with single-concentration hERGCentral screening data at 10 micromolar. ChEMBL records were restricted to direct single-target assignments (confidence 9), exact positive IC50 values in nM, and descriptions explicitly indicating electrophysiological measurements; flagged, potentially duplicated, mutant, binding-displacement, and indirect-readout records were excluded. Structures were standardized to nonstereochemical parent tautomer representations. Standardized SMILES were reparsed with default RDKit sanitization before deduplication and splitting. When uncharging produced unreadable SMILES, a validated charge-retaining parent tautomer was used; unresolved failures were excluded. All affected inputs were audited. Quantitative replicates were summarized by median pIC50, excluding groups spanning more than 1.0 log units or disagreeing across pIC50=5. Screen labels were assigned from median inhibition (>70% blocker; <30% nonblocker), excluding intermediate or conflicting groups. Exact quantitative data took precedence for overlapping structures; only additional compounds supplied screen-only labels. Both LightGBM models used radius-2 2048-bit Morgan fingerprints, 167 MACCS keys, and six RDKit physicochemical descriptors. The MSE comparator used measured compounds only; custom MTL additionally used screening-only compounds through its class-count-weighted squared hinge penalty. Nested 5-fold outer/3-fold inner random cross-validation used identical splits and 30 optimization trials per model, with 500 boosting rounds. MSE tuning minimized measured-target MSE; MTL tuning minimized MSE and maximized balanced accuracy, selecting the normalized Pareto point nearest the ideal. The classification boundary was fixed at pIC50=5; alpha was fixed at 1, beta was sampled log-uniformly from 0.5 to 5000.0, and the hinge buffer was sampled from 0.15 to 0.35 in 0.02 increments. Classification was evaluated on all held-out compounds and separately by label source; regression was evaluated on measured compounds only. Average precision summarized precision-recall performance. Results were reported per fold and from pooled out-of-fold predictions. This benchmark retains cross-assay heterogeneity and does not equate screening readouts with measured IC50 values.
