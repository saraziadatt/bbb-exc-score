hERG model comparison

Positive class: hERG blocker. Predictions >= 5 pIC50 are classified as blockers. Difference = mtl minus mse. CI: paired percentile bootstrap, 2,000 resamples stratified by fold, label source and class. P-values: two-sided paired prediction-swap permutation tests, 2,000 randomizations. Holm correction is applied across all classification metrics and subsets together, and separately across measured-subset regression metrics. NA denotes an undefined estimate/test. N is the eligible subset size; wrong-side distance uses each model's own misclassified compounds (counts are in counts.csv). CIs are conditional on these fitted out-of-fold models; training-set and chemical-series dependence are not captured. Degenerate bootstrap intervals can understate uncertainty.
