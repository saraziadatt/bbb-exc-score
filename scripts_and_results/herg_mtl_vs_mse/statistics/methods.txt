Saved out-of-fold predictions from the MSE and custom MTL hERG models were paired by compound identifier, with matching fold assignments, labels, and measured targets verified before analysis. No models were retrained. Classification used a fixed pIC50 threshold of 5 (blocker: prediction >= threshold) and was evaluated on all 282,155 compounds and separately on the measured-IC50 and screening-only subsets. Regression metrics used only the 2,815 measured pIC50 targets. Point estimates were calculated from pooled out-of-fold predictions; per-fold estimates and their mean and sample standard deviation were also reported. Model-specific estimates and paired differences (mtl minus mse) received 95% percentile confidence intervals from 2,000 paired compound bootstrap samples stratified jointly by outer fold, label source, and observed class. Two-sided permutation tests independently exchanged the two predictions within each compound pair over 2,000 randomizations under a null of within-pair prediction exchangeability. The p-value was (b+1)/(B+1), where b counted absolute randomized differences at least as large as the observed absolute difference, with floating-point tolerance. Raw p-values and Holm-adjusted values were reported, with all classification metric/subset comparisons forming one family and all measured-subset regression comparisons forming a second family. Average precision and trapezoidal PR AUC were reported separately. Precision is undefined when there are no predicted positives; undefined metrics are stored as NA, and valid bootstrap counts are reported. A permutation p-value is withheld if any randomized value is undefined. Wrong-side distance is the mean absolute distance from the threshold among each model's own misclassified compounds; it is not a measurement of error against the observed pIC50. Mean distance across all predictions is descriptive and has no universal preferred direction. Resampling inference is conditional on the fitted models and observed strata sizes and does not account for variation from retraining, overlapping cross-validation training sets, or dependence within chemical series. Degenerate bootstrap intervals, particularly for boundary-valued rates, can understate uncertainty.
