Statistical comparisons used the saved ensemble predictions for 43 compounds from the Kp,uu transfer evaluation, without retraining, new inference, or threshold selection. Predicted logBB was compared with log10(Kp,uu) using Spearman correlation, Pearson correlation, and Kendall tau-b. Low unbound exposure was defined as Kp,uu < 0.3. ROC AUC, average precision, and trapezoidal precision-recall AUC used negative predicted logBB as the positive-class score. Classification metrics used each model's previously saved logBB decision threshold. Percentile 95% confidence intervals for metrics and paired between-model differences were obtained using 2,000 paired compound bootstrap resamples. Two-sided paired permutation tests used 10,000 random within-compound swaps of the two models' prediction outputs, preserving the score and its corresponding binary classification together. P-values were computed as (1 + the number of permuted absolute differences at least as large as the observed absolute difference) / (1 + the number of permutations). These tests assume within-compound exchangeability of the model outputs under the null. Raw p-values and Holm-adjusted p-values across all 42 pairwise metric tests were reported; additional family-specific Holm adjustments were supplied separately for correlation and classification comparisons. Uncertainty was conditional on the fitted models and fixed thresholds. Undefined precision and F1 were set to zero; constant-prediction MCC was zero when both target classes were present. Undefined bootstrap statistics were excluded with valid replicate counts reported. Tests with undefined permutation statistics were flagged rather than assigned a numerical p-value, except that a zero observed difference was assigned p = 1.
